### Juggernaut Exploration Ltd.

Reports petrographic study results for the Big One Au Project, Tlēgōhīn (Telegraph Creek), BC

<div class="note-block">
<strong>Note:</strong><br>

Photomicrographs lack a legible scale. 'High gold grades observed in the veins' and 'up to' lack assay values or ranges. 'Geochemical trace element' lacks element names. 'Remains wide open' lacks geological context. Grab sample lacks type (outcrop, float). AuEq is applied to non-drill core samples. 'Polymetallic veins, alteration signature, geochemical pathfinder element signature, and geophysical anomalies' lacks metrics. 'District scale' and 'System covers an area of 22 km2' lacks drilling and an MRE. AuEq uses borrowed recoveries: 'Recoveries for Au, Ag, Cu, Pb, and Zn on Big One are not known.' CO2 bearing inclusions and propylitic alteration are consistent with magmatic fluids, but do not by themselves confirm a porphyry system. ‘No deleterious elements’ and ‘clean geochemistry’ lack metallurgy tests. 'Free milling' lacks communition tests. Vein counting methodology, 4-acid sample weight and QA/QC verification results are missing.

</div>

[News Release](https://juggernautexploration.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/JUGGERNAUT-NEWS-RELEASE-Feb-23-2026.pdf)
