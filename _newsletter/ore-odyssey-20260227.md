### Volt Carbon Technologies Inc.

Provides a technical update on the historical geochemical data and recent metallurgical work from the Mount Copeland Mo-REE and the Red Bird Projects, Revelstoke, Burns Lake, BC

<div class="note-block">
<strong>Note:</strong><br>

'Up to' lacks assay values or ranges. 'MoS2 Equivalent' are applied to non-independent XRF results. Sample size/weight, prep & assay method, MoS2 Equivalent parameters & equation, and a QA/QC description are missing. 
XRF measures total elemental concentrations and does not distinguish between molybdenite (MoS2), wulfenite (PbMoO4), or powellite (CaMoO4). Converting to oxide and then to sulfide introduces conversion factor errors if the stoichiometric assumptions (e.g., that all Mo is 
MoS2) do not match the mineralogy. In metallurgical processing, converting sulfides to oxides is common, but in exploration geology, assuming an oxide-to-sulfide conversion can lead to  overestimation of ore grade if the mineral is already oxidized.

</div>
