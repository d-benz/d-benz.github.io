### CopAur Minerals Inc.

Provides a summary of Omega Pacific's Exploratory Data Analysis on the Williams Cu-Au Project, Tatl’ah (Dease Lake), BC

<div class="note-block">
<strong>Note:</strong><br>

CopAur:
'Confirming a coherent, structurally controlled gold system' lacks metrics and an MRE. 'Open' lacks geological context. 'Strong correlation' lacks a method. 'Gold grades' and 'soil anomalies' lacks assay values or ranges. EDA methodology is missing.

Omega Pacific:
'Clearly define an extensive gold system' and 'confirm GIC Prospect hosts a coherent, structurally controlled gold system' lack an MRE. 'Gold mineralization', 'compelling soil geochemical anomalies', 'copper-molybdenum and gold-bismuth-tellurium signatures', 'gold grades', 'exceeding', 'higher-grade', and 'copper-molybdenum and gold-bismuth-tellurium anomalies' lack assay values or ranges. 'Remains open' and 'open in all directions' lack geological context. Figures 1 - 3 Rock lack sample type (outcrop, float). Figure 2 lacks an assay results legend. 'Logged pyrite abundances greater than 15% are associated with an estimated greater than 80% probability of gold grades exceeding' lacks methodology. Soil horizon, soil sample depth, soil sample spacing, soil sample interpolation method, sample weight, core size, core sample length, true width, collar table, prep & assay method, correlation & clustering methods, and a QA/QC description are missing.
