<strong>Note:</strong><br>

'Widely spaced', 'multiple', 'broad', 'shallow', 'abundant', 'at depth', 'significant', 'high sulfide contents', 'anomalous indicator elements', and 'widespread' lack metrics. 'Copper-gold mineralization', 'strongly anomalous', 'elevated', and 'up to' lack assay values or ranges. 'Over a 4 km2 area' and 'largest and most underexplored Cu-Au systems' lack an MRE. PyEq is based on stoichiometric pyrite FeS2 where it can overestimate pyrite if some sulfur is in other sulfides (like chalcopyrite or arsenopyrite) and when it assumes that 100 % of the measured S is in sulfide minerals while not accounting for sulfates. 'Length-weighted modelled pyrite content' method, pyrite content to chargeability correlation methodology, 4-acid assay weight, and QA/QC verification results are missing. Intervals are without a Maximum Internal Dilution and a minimum grade.
